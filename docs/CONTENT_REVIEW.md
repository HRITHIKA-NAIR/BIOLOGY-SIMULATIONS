## Required next review, practical by practical

1. Obtain viewable video or a user-supplied permitted copy/transcript. Watch the actual demonstration, including actions not spoken aloud.
2. For every stage record start/end timestamps, apparatus identity, quantities/units, action, waiting duration, observation and safety instruction. No estimated timestamps labelled as observed.
3. Compare with current qualification specification and approved school practical guidance. Record disagreements. Do not blindly reproduce an error in a video.
4. Choose and document the exact method variant. The photosynthesis indicator method and pondweed gas/bubble method are not interchangeable; onion and cheek-cell slide preparations differ.
5. Build a detailed original storyboard, narration/captions, accessible written procedure and PDF from the same versioned content. Check each visually against the stage evidence.

## Modelling boundaries

No numerical experimental model is claimed. Indicator colours, balance masses, capillary movement, cell illustration, landscape and inhibition zones are pedagogical examples, not measured video results or calibrated predictions. No numerical pH is inferred from a schematic colour. Inhibition-zone size is affected by diffusion and dose and is not a clinical ranking.

Source PDFs carry copyright notices. They are linked, not bundled. Do not add third-party transcripts, screenshots, audio, original worksheets or permission correspondence to the public repository. Keep rights evidence in a genuinely private store.
