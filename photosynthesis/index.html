Practical notes & sources

This original animated teaching demonstration uses a simplified saturating model: 90 / (1 + (distance/20)²) bubbles per minute. Counts are simulated, not experimental observations. Temperature, pondweed and carbon dioxide availability are held constant. Bubble sizes vary in reality, so bubble count is only an approximate measure of oxygen production.

Each measurement represents 60 seconds shown in 12 seconds of playback. Adjustment is abbreviated for teaching. Distances and timings are prototype choices; the full method in the reference video has not yet been verified. Watch advances automatically. Try pauses at lamp-placement stages. Drag the lamp, or select it then select its outline; Enter on the lamp is a keyboard alternative. Back/Next navigate the explanation and do not fabricate skipped results. Changing modes restarts the demonstration.
